Conference Presentations

 
     
  • ND EPSCoR Annual Conference, 2024
      The Influence of AGN Feedback on Star Formation in Spiral Galaxies  
      Multi-wavelength investigation of AGN feedback and its effects on star formation in spiral galaxies using SDSS, Chandra, XMM-Newton, and WISE observations.
    •  
     
  • 240th American Astronomical Society Meeting, 2022
      Characterization of Ionized Jet Candidates in High-Mass Star-Forming Regions Using VLASS   (iPoster)  
      Analysis of VLASS radio continuum observations of ionized jet candidates in high-mass star-forming regions, including radio variability studies, spectral-index analysis, and spectral energy distribution (SED) modelling.
    •  
     
  • Illinois State Academy of Science Annual Meeting, 2022
      Investigating the Spectral Energy Distribution of Young High-Mass Stellar Objects Using VLASS  
      Awarded 2nd Place in the student presentation competition. Research focused on SED analysis and radio properties of young high-mass stellar objects.
